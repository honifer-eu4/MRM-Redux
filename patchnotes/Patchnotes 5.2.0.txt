MRM Redux Patch 5.0.0:

############# General/Bugfixes ###############

Bukhara's dev cost mission now appears as intended
Counter-Reformation and Council of Trent should now always happen around 1550
Tunis's religion mods now give tolerance as intended instead of max tolerance
Maderia sugar cane is now a permanent province modifier 
Colonial nations can't do destroy estates
Morocco now has 10 HCA as intended and can take their salt mine mission before defeating Portugal
Ingerland can no longer spam gold in all of Mexico and Southern US, thanks Lorb
Removed Great Greek Revolt

################## Ages ######################

AI can no longer golden era

Age of Discovery:
    The former transfer subjects ability now allows for claim fabrication with no additional cost instead of 50% cheaper transfer of subjects in peace deals 
    Feudal Society objective now requires 4 subjects instead of 5

################ Mechanics ###################

Defines:
    Reduced supply depot cost to 5 mil instead of 20

Trade:
    Northern Mediterranean: 
        Combines Venice and Genoa into one trade node without the Provence state

    Southern Mediterranean:
        This is now an end node, it receives incoming trade from Alexandra, Sevilla, Valencia, and Katsina
        Malta TC moved to Messina

    Bordeaux: 
        Gained the Provence state

    Champagne:
        Added a trade center in Namur

    Rhineland:
        Removed the trade center in Mainz, added one in Rothenburg and Hanover

Map: 
    Changes to African cultures to reduce the rebel spam, Katsina added to the Solus Europa bookmark to compensate Tunis for Naples gaining Sicily
    Removed some vanilla autonomy modifiers from Berber provinces, added 3 dev to Fezzan
    Cyprus now starts with the Famagusta province modifier
    Reworked fort placement in Tunis and Naples to account for map changes
    Messina trade good changed to wine
    Added strait crossing between Cornwall and Penn-ar-Bed
    Cornwall is now a hills title (instead of coastline)
    Kent is now a march tile (instead of farmlands)
    Sirte is now a coastal desert tile (instead of desert)
    Oxford is now a farmlands tile (instead of grasslands)
    Coastline is now +0.1 devcost instead of +0.15

General:
    Minor buffs to Milan and Naple's province monuments
    Idea groups now unlocked at adm tech: 5, 7, 10, 13, 16, 19, 22, 25
